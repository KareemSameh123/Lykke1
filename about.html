Our Story

Mission & Vision

Modern heritage, made in Egypt. Here's what drives everything we design, cut, and stitch.

01

Our Mission

To design streetwear that honors Egyptian heritage while speaking a bold, modern language — giving a new generation clothing they're proud to wear and stories they're proud to tell. Every piece we release is made with intention, built to last, and priced to be worn, not shelved.

02

Our Vision

To become the definitive homegrown streetwear label to come out of Egypt — one that puts local craftsmanship on a global stage and proves that heritage and hype were never opposites. We see a future where LYKKE is worn far beyond where it started.

Crafted in Egypt

Every drop is designed and produced locally, start to finish.

Built to Last

Quality fabrics and construction over disposable fast fashion.

Modern Heritage

Vintage aesthetics reworked for the streets of today.
